    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Work the stages in order. The story comes first because it unlocks the world and the side strands; the money trophies come last because robberies and heists fund them. Each stage lists its trophies — tick them off in the Checklist as you go.

      Total: 55 trophies. The platinum, “Vice Got a Price!”, unlocks on its own once the other 54 are earned. The second gold, “A Long Road” (100% completion), is the true endgame — leave it until everything else is done and use the in-game completion tracker for the last few percent.

      Missables — read this before the finale

      Honest note: the official trophy descriptions do not say any trophy is permanently missable. What they do say is that 17 trophies each name one chapter or one mission. We flag those as story-gated — treat as missable: earn each one while you are on that chapter or mission, and finish all five side-character strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) before the final mission. Everything else in the list is repeatable in free roam after the story.
